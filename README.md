## Hi, I'm Daniel 👋

CTO at Insurance Nation, AI engineer the rest of the time.

I build operational systems that survive contact with messy reality. Most of my
work takes software (and lately, AI) out of the demo stage and into workflows

remove the steps no one should be doing by hand.

Insurance turns out to be a good proving ground. Forms, endorsements, carrier
docs: none of it is clean, so most of what I build has to survive that reality
before it counts.
